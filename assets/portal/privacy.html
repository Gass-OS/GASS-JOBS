Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This page explains how the TUN-JOBS website and account portal handle information. TUN-JOBS is a project by GASS OS intended to connect job seekers and employers.

1. Information you provide

When you create an account or complete your profile, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, city, profession, education, skills, availability, profile photo and CV. Your password is handled by Supabase Authentication; TUN-JOBS does not intentionally store your password in its own website code or profile fields.

2. Why information is used

  • Create and secure your account.
  • Save and display the professional information you choose to provide.
  • Support candidate and employer features, where enabled.
  • Respond to support, security and account-deletion requests.

3. Photos, CVs and visibility

Profile photos are uploaded to the candidate-photos storage bucket, which is configured as public in the current project. Do not upload a photo that you do not want publicly accessible. CV files are uploaded to the candidate-cvs private bucket. The website does not create a public CV link. Access to private files depends on the storage policies configured in Supabase.

Never assume that a CV, phone number or email address is visible only to a particular audience unless the relevant database and storage access policies have been configured and tested. The public website currently does not provide a public directory of candidate records.

4. Service providers and hosting

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Account authentication and profile metadata are handled by the Supabase project configured for TUN-JOBS. Uploaded photos and CVs are stored in Supabase Storage. These providers process data under their own service terms and privacy documentation.

5. Retention and deletion

Your account information and uploaded files may remain stored until you delete them or request deletion. Account deletion must be handled through the project operator and Supabase administration; the current static portal does not yet perform self-service account deletion. When requesting deletion, identify the account email and ask for deletion of the authentication account, profile data, photo and CV objects.

6. Your choices

Provide only information needed for your use of the service. Keep your CV and contact information current, avoid uploading sensitive personal documents, and contact the project operator if you want access, correction or deletion of your information.

7. Security

We aim to use Supabase authentication and access policies to protect accounts and private files. No online service can guarantee absolute security. See the Security page for practical guidance and reporting information.

8. Children

TUN-JOBS is a professional employment service and is not intended for children.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as features change. For questions or privacy requests, contact the project through the GASS-JOBS GitHub repository. This notice is an initial project policy, not legal advice. The operator should review it for applicable Tunisian and European data-protection requirements before public launch.
